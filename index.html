Zijian Li 李子健

Understanding how people learn, reason, and solve complex problems with AI.探索人们如何在人工智能支持下学习、推理与解决复杂问题。

I am a master's student in Educational Technology at Peking University, advised by Prof. Yizhou Fan. My research connects learning sciences with computational methods to examine how learners interact with AI. My interests include LLM-based learner simulation, learning analytics, complex problem solving, and how AI feedback influences reasoning and self-regulated learning.

我是北京大学教育学院教育技术系硕士研究生，导师为范逸洲老师。我的研究结合学习科学与计算方法，关注学习者与 AI 的交互过程，包括基于大语言模型的学习者模拟、学习分析、复杂问题解决，以及 AI 反馈如何影响推理与自我调节学习。

LLM-Based Learner Simulation for Complex Problem Solving

基于大语言模型的复杂问题解决学习者模拟

Exploring API-based LLM agents for simulating variation in learners' problem-solving processes, without fine-tuning. Current work focuses on learner-role settings and process-level validation.

探索在不微调模型的情况下，使用 API 调用的大语言模型模拟不同学习者的复杂问题解决过程。目前重点研究学习者角色设定与过程层面的模拟验证。

Manuscript in preparation论文写作中

Hypothesis-Space Development in AI-Supported Clinical Reasoning

AI 支持的临床推理中假设空间的发展

Examining 385 learner–case trajectories from 121 medical students, using ranked diagnostic hypotheses, textual evidence, semantic embeddings, and feedback records to study hypothesis revisions as information unfolds.

分析来自 121 名医学生的 385 条任务轨迹，将诊断假设排序、文本证据、语义向量与 AI 反馈记录结合起来，研究新信息逐步呈现时假设如何变化。

Reliable LLM Coding and Human–AI Collaborative Learning Analytics

可靠的 LLM 编码与人机协同学习分析

Comparing LLMs and smaller classifiers for theory-driven dialogue coding, identifying systematic errors in rare categories, and evaluating a human-in-the-loop coding workflow.

比较大语言模型和小型分类模型在理论驱动对话编码中的表现，分析稀有类别的系统性错误，并评估人机协作编码流程。
